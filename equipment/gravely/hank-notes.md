## Answers (11/5)

### Brakes
Buy replacement brake lining material from [McMaster-Carr](https://www.mcmaster.com/) and glue it on with a "something-Weld" epoxy. That's probably J-B Weld, but confirm with Hank.

### Snow plow lift rod
Buy a 12" bolt and cut the head off. Put a 90° bend in it and drill a hole for the cotter pin.

### Paint (panels)
1. Sand the panel smooth.
2. Prime with a brown primer from a brand Rust-Oleum owns. This is probably Rust-Oleum's red-oxide (reddish-brown) metal primer, but confirm the exact product with Hank.
3. Top coat: Ford tractor red. Rust-Oleum Farm & Implement makes Ford Red in spray, quart and gallon.
4. If spraying, **don't thin with naphtha**. It kills the gloss.

### Hydraulic pump
It's fine to remove the hydraulic pump and make a plate to cover the hole.

### Fuel shutoff valve
The gas valve goes **before** the filter and fuel pump, not after the pump.

## Follow-up research (not from Hank)

### Starter groan (research)
**Symptom:** the starter groans as it disengages. The usual causes on Onan-type starters, cheapest check first:
1. Dirty or dry Bendix drive shaft, so the pinion drags coming off the flywheel. Clean it and put on a light coat of grease ([Wheel Horse forum, Onan 16hp](https://www.wheelhorseforum.com/topic/95804-onan-starter-hanging-up)).
2. Weak or broken Bendix return spring.
3. Worn nose bushing in the starter housing, so the armature wobbles.
4. Missing alignment shims, if the starter has been off before.
5. Damaged teeth on the flywheel ring gear or the pinion.
6. Sticky solenoid that keeps the starter running a moment after the key is released.

If cleaning and greasing don't fix it, rebuild it (Chestnut Hill Auto Electric?) or replace it.

### Long PTO (research)
**Plan:** extend the existing front PTO shaft. It isn't square, so it's probably hex.
- I found no published hex size for the G series. Measure across the flats with calipers, and measure the cross-pin or set-screw hole diameter and spacing.
- Gravely front-attachment PTO shafts are listed as shared across the 8179, 8199 and 12G–20G ([eBay listing](https://www.ebay.de/itm/277533222870)). The 8000-series parts tractor may have a donor shaft.
- Stock: hex bar in 1045 or 4140 steel from McMaster-Carr in the measured size. Hex bar is sized across the flats.
- A forum consensus on PTO extensions is to get a longer shaft rather than an add-on extension ([TractorByNet](https://www.tractorbynet.com/forums/threads/how-far-can-you-extend-a-pto-shaft.209517/page-3)).
