# Gravely 8000 series vs Professional G series: parts interchange

## What did NOT carry over

**Forward-reverse clutch friction parts (the "new style" clutch):**

| Part | 8000 (0506, 1985) | G (0227, 1987 → 1995) |
|---|---|---|
| Clutch disc / assembly | 38932 Assembly, Clutch Disc | 42482 Assembly, Clutch and Ring; 42484 Disc, Clutch; 42481 Ring, Clutch |
| Lining | 38931 Lining, Clutch | 40692 Lining, Clutch |
| Trunnion, bearing, spring pin, cotter pin | 34734, 35330, 35681, 121223 | **same** |

The G clutch keeps the 8000 trunnion and bearings but uses different friction parts. Whether the new-style clutch fits an older 8000 trunnion isn't stated in these manuals. Ask Hank or a dealer before mixing them.

**Also G-only:**
- Engines and exhaust: 12 hp electric-start, 16 hp Magnum, 18 hp, 20 hp, with their mufflers and headers.
- The 4-wheel frame weldment (40918) and mower brackets (21087 / 21088).
- Instrument panel and support (43799, 41240), wiring harness (41865), magnetic switch (35634).
- Hydraulic control rod (41237 / 41239) and PTO knob (32659).
- Front PTO cap (18859) and axle bearing retainer (42976).
- Reverse clutch spring (43789), oil seal 15137, steering plastic tube (40984).
- Hood (43802) and all G decals (43244–43247 model decals, 43003 logo, 44074 side stripe).

## Takeaways for the $50 8000-series parts tractor

- **Transmission internals, PTO clutch, differential, brakes, steering, wheels and the lift are good donors.** They're nearly all the same part numbers.
- **Fwd-rev clutch friction discs and linings are different.** Use the parts tractor's clutch only after comparing it with the 16-G's.
- **Engine, exhaust, wiring, dash and hood are not interchangeable.**
- The closer the parts tractor is to 1985–86 ("8000-G"), the better the match. Get its model # from the plate under the hood on the right side.

## Later G years

The May 2001 catalog (0528) uses Ariens numbering. Old 5-digit numbers appear with a leading zero (e.g. 017945), and new parts have 8-digit numbers. Of its 511 unique part numbers, 217 also appear in the 1987 list. So late-production 16-Gs differ more from the 8000 series than early ones. Check your tractors' build years.
