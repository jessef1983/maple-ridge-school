            FOR THE 40", 5011 AND 60" MOWERS
        To Install Mower Lift Pivot To Tractor, Do The Following:
        I.)  Lower attachment lift arm. If tractor has manual lift, put in lowest post ion.
             If tractor has hydraulic lift, hold hydraulic control handle in down position
             with engine running until lift arm is down.
        2.)  Insert mower lift pivot (Item I) through the appropriate tear drop shaped hole
             in the lift arm for your particular attachment.
        3.)  Retain mower lift pivot with a hair pin cotter (Item 2).
