                  INSTALLATION INSTRUCTION
                  19134E1 Rear PTO
                  Form No. 19568P1
                  Effective Date: 5-1-72
                  The gear box support, as shown in fig 1 and described in paragraphs 3 and
                  8 of the instructions, has been replaced with a "hook" type support-bracket;
                  shown as Item 22, Illustrated Parts List, Form No. 20935L1, and as found
                  in the hardware pack.
                  Replace paragraph 3 of the instructions with the following procedure.
                  3. Hook the support-bracket over the clutch cross-shaft of The Gravely.
                      Position it between the spring-clamp assembly and the frame. Have
                     the short end towards the rear as shown by the Illustrated Parts List.
                  Disregard paragraph 8. The "hook" must be removed when the gear box is
                  removed after use.
