# Gravely attachment manuals

These 203 PDFs come from oldgravelys.net. They cover both riding-tractor and walk-behind attachments.

- **PDFs:** they live in SharePoint (`Shared Documents/gravely-tractor-kb/manuals/attachments`), not in git.
- **`md/`:** OCR'd Markdown for the **105 attachments relevant to the riding tractors**: snowdozers and blades, 38" and larger snow blowers, 40–72" mower decks and boot kits, front drive, adapter and mount kits, and rear PTO. The rest are PDF-only. If an attachment turns out to be relevant, OCR it.
- **Parts:** parts tables from these manuals, including the parts lists printed inside operator's manuals, are merged into [../parts-index.csv](../parts-index.csv).
