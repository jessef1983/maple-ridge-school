# Session Summary — Sugarhouse evaporator arch rebuild plan

Started in `maple-ridge-music-program` as "MPR Sap Arch revamp". Filed here because the sap operation is school work. No code or repo files changed; this note is the only record of the plan, which is still active.

## Pickup / goal

Jesse pasted a Gemini blueprint for rebuilding the 3' wide wood-fired evaporator arch and asked for gaps. It was reviewed against on-site measurements and photos taken the same day, and ended in a build plan and shopping list. The photos were conversation attachments only and are not in the repo.

## Decisions

- **Natural draft only.** Forced air is ruled out, so the Gemini fan, manifold and door kill switch are dropped.
- **Air control at the intake, no stack damper:** slides on the two ash openings, plus a passive overfire slide in the fire door.
- **No tall bridge wall, no rear pocket, no wedge.** The Gemini ramp numbers contradicted the 7" flues.
- **Keep the stack.** Four Selkirk Metalbestos Ultra-Temp 12UT-36 sections, insulated, 2" clearance, with an 11.75" ID single-wall stub clamped to the hood. Jesse confirmed all labels match and cupola clearance meets standards.
- **Keep the fiberboard in the firebox.** Jesse asked to stop tracking its ID. The plan does not cut or fasten into it.
- **Stop tracking season totals.** About 1,200 taps; Jesse said they boil as much as they need.
- **Brick:** full 2.5" in the firebox, 1.25" splits elsewhere, rigid board behind the rear-wall splits if the fin test below passes.
- **Door glass:** ceramic stove glass about 8×10 in a bolted steel frame. Not tempered glass, and not a whole stove door.

## Measurements (inches, stations from firebox front)

- Rim 32.5 above floor. Grate 25.5 below rim, 7 above floor. Firebox 36 deep. Inside rim 34.5 × 113.75.
- Firebox inside width 29 at base. Center support 36.5 × 7 × 3/8. Grate bars 27.75 long, 10 total, 3.5 on center, 2" angle set V-up.
- Pans: finish 1–37, flue 37–97 (7" flues running the full length, front to back), filtration 97–113 (4" flues).
- Filtration pan outlet 32 × 3.5, starting at rim height (112 sq in; stack 108 sq in).
- Floor: slopes up over 9" to 21 below rim at 45.5, about 2 1/8" per foot back to 8.5 below rim at 115.
- Angles: at 59 (top 15 below rim) and 87.75 (top 11.25 below rim). Plate 12.5 below rim at the 8' mark. The 11.25 and 12.5 direct readings replaced earlier calculated values.
- Stack starts 75 above rim, top about 251.5 off the floor, 12' of pipe.
- Building: 155.5 interior width, 359 interior length. Arch appears to sit about 54 from each side wall; that reading is inferred. Shelf behind it is being removed.

## Build plan

1. Close the air bypass. The ash pit back wall has two burned slots open to the room under the shell, and a strip above it opens into the gas path. Together they let air skip the fire, the likely cause of the black smoke after stoking and the creosote. Fit 3/16" cover plates, then a mortared firebrick wall 29 wide × 7 tall, kept clear of the rear grate support.
2. Replace the jog and the bay to 59 with new plate, and line with 1" blanket plus 2.5" brick up to the top of the first angle.
3. From 59 back, leave the old flimsy plates as a tray for blanket filling the void. Set new 3/16" trapezoid plates on top of the angles, the rear one running to a new ledge about 1.3" below the rear lip. Tack only, seal the edges, and lay 1.25" splits on top.
4. Target clearance under fins: 6.5 at 59, 2.8 at 87.75, 2.5 at 97, 4.5 under the filtration fins at 113. Aim for 2.5–3 at the rear, never under 2. Dry-fit before mortaring.
5. Light-test from underneath in a dark arch before any lining goes on.
6. Grates: 10 bars of 2×2×1/4 angle at 27.5, new center support, keep the ash-filled V-up setup.
7. Add door and rail gaskets, new door liner, glass, and a stack thermometer in or just above the hood.

## Budget

About $2,330–$4,306 with 15% contingency, excluding welding labor, tax, shipping and any stack hardware. Steel, gasket and glass prices are estimates. Quantities: 1 sheet 3/16" plate (tight fit), 2 × 20' angle, 72 full firebrick, 106 splits, 1 roll 50 sq ft 1" blanket, 3 sheets 1" rigid board, 2 × 50 lb refractory mortar, 2 × 25' rail gasket.

## Still open / next

- Measure fin spans, and channel width 7" below rim at 59, 88, 97 and 4" below rim at 105, 113. Test: width at fin height minus 4.5 must be at least fin span plus 1.
- Measure plate-level widths at each angle and the rear lip, and the clear lengths between angles, to cut the plates.
- Measure firebox side ledge width. It needs about 2.5, and brick must not rest on grate bars. Check logs against about 24 at the base.
- Measure the door for the liner, and lay out cuts on the one plate sheet.
- Confirm the 12UT-36 temperature listing with Selkirk/DuraVent, and ask the local building or fire official whether residential-type chimney is acceptable on an evaporator. Sources disagreed on the 10–14" listing, and no NY evaporator rule was found.
- Confirm stack joint bands, stack support (not on the hood), hood top reaching 75 above rim, stub retention, and 18" from the stub to timber and sap line.
- Bath: fill-test the riveted hood seam before firing, and confirm the sight tube reads bath level.
- Write a low-sap procedure with a reserve feed. Sealing makes the slides a stronger brake, but brick holds heat, so sap must keep flowing during cooldown.

## Caveats

- 80–125 GPH is an estimate for natural draft, not a measured rate. Budget a tuning period at the test fire.
- Running the rear with splits leaves little margin. Rear channel area at 2.5" is around 80 sq in plus the channels between fins.
- The fiberboard's material was never identified. This was raised several times; Jesse asked to drop it. Earlier advice was to test before disturbing it.
- Rough rule-of-thumb sizing gave about 12,000 gal of sap, 300 gal of syrup, and 8–12 cords per season at 1,200 taps. Storage for a peak day may be the first capacity limit. RO status is unknown.
- Galvanized panels mean zinc fumes when cutting or welding. Blanket and board work is adult-only with N95s. Student roles were never defined.
- The synced `industrial-arts` Claude skill (user-level, not in this repo) has no researched sap section, and nothing from this session was added to it.
