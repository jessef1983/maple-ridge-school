# 2026-11-16: Sap house extension post layout and north elevation

## Pickup / goal
Jesse asked for a floor plan for post placement, then a north elevation that shows both phases.

## Completed (commit 10edbd6, branch `session-2026-11-16-sap-house-layout`)
- **`sap-house/extension/post_layout.py`** generates `post-layout.html` and `.pdf`.
  - Page 1 is a 3/16" plan of the post and footing centers:
    - Phase 1 awning A1–A4: 6'-0" off the east face, at 7'-3⅝" centers.
    - Phase 2 porch front N1–N4: 8'-0" off the north face.
    - Phase 2 back posts N5–N7: tight to the north siding.
  - Page 2 is a stake-out table: face-line offsets plus two corner tape distances per post.
    Diagonal checks are 33'-0½" for the building and 20'-10⅝" for NW→N4 = NE→N1.
- **`sap-house/extension/north_elevation.py`** generates `north-elevation.html` and `.pdf`.
  - Two pages at 1/4" = 1'-0": after Phase 1, and after Phase 2. Looking south, so east is on
    the left.
- Both read their geometry from `make_awning_scr.py`, the single source of truth.
