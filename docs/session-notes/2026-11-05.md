## Findings and caveats

- **Second plate number is probably not the model #.** 16-G model numbers in owner posts are 6 digits starting with 987 (987070, 987061). The model tag may be under the front hood, on the dash support. Give model # + serial # to a dealer to get the build year. I found no public serial-to-year decoder.
- **Tires:**
  - The stock rear is 23x8.50-12. The Dunlop KT155 22x10-9 from FB needs its own 9" wheels, so the hub bolt pattern decides whether it works (not yet measured).
  - The front Kenda AT 20x7-8 fits the stock 8" front rim. It's 4" taller than the stock 16x6.50-8, so the front sits about 2" higher. Check steering-lock clearance and how the deck hangs.
- **8000-series parts unit:** The G series replaced the 8000 series in 1987, and both have 8F/4R transmissions. A direct swap of the F/R clutch is **not confirmed**. Get the parts unit's model #. A late 8000 (8163/8179/8199) is the likeliest match.
- **Seat:** Gravely sells cushion parts (21560800, 21557800, 59204300), but none are confirmed for the 16-G. Fallbacks: an upholsterer, or a universal seat.
- **Decals:** No reproduction G-series set found. Photograph and measure every decal **before** the burn-off/repaint.
- **Plow lift rod:** Hank's 12" bolt (head cut off, 90° bend, cotter pin) appears to be the same part as the spring-loaded rod on Brandon's tractor, made more cheaply.
- **Angle lever:** The recommendation is to fab it, unless the parts unit or Brandon has the original. The angle mechanism hasn't been seen yet.
- **Hank's contacts:**
  - Dick Redinsky (club) and Chestnut Hill Auto Electric weren't findable online.
  - "Jacks engine MN" is probably Jack's Small Engines.
  - A Hank Dermer is a Gravely Tractor Club of America director. It might be the same Hank; unconfirmed.
