# 2026-11-06: Gravely manuals KB + plow angle handle

## Pickup / goal

This session continued the Gravely 16-G work from 2026-11-05. Jesse picked up the $50 8000-series parts tractor and asked three things:
- how many parts interchange between the 8000 and Professional G series;
- ingest every Gravely tractor and attachment manual and publish them to a Gravely KB on the school SharePoint;
- work out how to fab the snow plow's angle lever.

## Completed

- **Manual ingest:** `equipment/gravely/manuals/`, commit `3cf8ed9`.
  - 30 tractor manuals (8000, 9000 and Professional G series) and 203 attachment manuals.
  - The 105 attachment manuals relevant to the riding tractors were OCR'd. The rest are PDF-only.
  - One Markdown file per manual in `md/`, raw text in `text/`, and `parts-index.csv` (about 13,400 rows).
  - `8000-vs-G-interchange.md`:
    - 89% of the 1987 G part numbers also appear in 8000 lists (87% excluding hardware).
    - The F/R clutch friction parts differ (8000: 38932/38931; G: 42482/42484/42481/40692). Trunnion and bearings are the same.
    - Engine, exhaust, wiring, dash and hood are G-only.
  - **PDFs are git-ignored** (`manuals/.gitignore` = `*.pdf`) and live in SharePoint. Git LFS was considered and not used.
  - How the text was made: the PDF's own text layer where usable, otherwise Windows' built-in OCR (`Windows.Media.Ocr`) at 200 dpi, with table rows rebuilt from word positions. The scripts are in the session scratchpad (`winocr.ps1`, `ocr_all.py`, `build_md.py`) and are **not saved to the repo**.
  - The oldgravelys.net filenames call 0512–0514 "G Series", but the covers say 9000 Series. They're filed as 9000.
- **SharePoint Gravely KB:** https://ccfedu.sharepoint.com/sites/MPRSchool, `Shared Documents/gravely-tractor-kb`.
  - 385 files uploaded, 0 failures: 13 at the root, 33 in `manuals`, 30 in `manuals/md`, 204 in `attachments`, 105 in `attachments/md`.
  - Search finds the .md body text (for example "Weldment, Rail Shift Fork").
  - Auth: Jesse's B2B guest account (`ccimail`), Windows PowerShell 5.1, PnP.PowerShell 1.12.0, `-UseWebLogin`. The guest can add and edit items but can't manage lists.
  - Upload script `gravely-kb-upload.ps1` (modes DryRun/Folders/Pilot/Bulk, resumable) is in the scratchpad only.
- **Earlier publish now recorded:** the logo package was published to `Shared Documents/maple-ridge-logo-resources` on the same site in an earlier session, but never written into session notes.
- **Plow angle handle:** `equipment/gravely/README.md` has a new "Angle lever" section with the mechanism, which parts are on hand, and a fab plan.
  - Snowdozer 24678 parts list: 0650 p.10 (also 0647).
  - Jesse's 6 photos show every part except the handle (18503) and grip (11861).
  - Decided to fab rather than buy eBay #127579943461 ($73 shipped, looks shop-made), using a 2-plate clevis.
  - Purchases table updated: parts tractor bought, total now $1,320.

## Findings and caveats

- OCR'd part numbers aren't perfect. Check against the PDF page before ordering.
- The parts tractor's model # still isn't known. The closer it is to 1985–86 ("8000-G"), the better the parts match.
- The plow ball-joint README text is unchanged from PR #5 ("about 9/16"). Jesse later said the socket is "just under 9/16" and a 1/2" ball is probably right. The edit that would have recorded this was declined, so it isn't in the README.
- The fab dimensions are estimates until Jesse measures the stub and the cross-hole.
