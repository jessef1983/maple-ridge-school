# 2026-11-27 / 11-28: Sap house Phase 1 awning footers (purchasing + pour plan)

## Pickup / goal
Jesse asked about the "west awning footers". In the plan this is the **Phase 1 east awning**,

## Decided / purchased
- **Concrete:** bought **38 × 60 lb Sakrete High Strength** (about 4000 psi) at Lowe's. Quikrete
  80 lb was sold out, and Lowe's could not deliver until Dec 7 (holiday backlog).
  - Volume per footer: about 3.4 cu ft (22" × 6" base 1.3 + 12" tube about 32" long 2.1).
    About 13.6 cu ft total, or about 31 × 60 lb bags. The 38 includes about 25% spare.
  - Reference counts for the same spare margin: 45 × 50 lb, 38 × 60 lb, 30 × 80 lb.
- **Rebar:** bought **8 × 10' #4**. Not in the original plan, which uses Anti-Crak fiber in the
  tubes (11/3 field notes). Planned use is 3 L-bars per footer, about 40", with a 6–8" foot in the
  base and 3" cover, in a triangle about 6" across. That uses about 40'; the remainder is spare
  for cross bars in the bases or Phase 2.
- **Two-stage pour** (Jesse's plan, a change from the engineer's 11/4 detail of one continuous
  pour with the tube hung above the hole bottom):
  1. Pour the base with the rebar set, about 3 bags per footer. Set the tube on it at the
     correct height, plumb and centered on the stake-out mark. Leave the top rough.
  2. Fill the tube, about 5 bags per footer. Before filling, clean the base and dampen it with
     no standing water. Set the anchor bolt in the center, rod the concrete, and brace the tube
     at grade.
  - The cold joint is acceptable because the rebar crosses it. Tell the engineer about the change.
