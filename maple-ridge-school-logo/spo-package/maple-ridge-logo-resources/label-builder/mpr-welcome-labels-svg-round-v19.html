Welcome Label Builder

Avery 94510 round labels (2¼", 12 per sheet). The eagle, leaves and circles are locked artwork. Labels are clipped to the true 2¼-inch round shape so you can see how they sit on the Avery template. The top and bottom lettering is live SVG text, so it can be changed without changing the artwork.

Label text

Text follows the original curved logo layout. Long names are automatically reduced to fit rather than truncated.

Logo center & zoom — eagle / maple leaves positioning

Center X/Y fine-tune the eagle and leaves within the label circle (positive moves right/down). Zoom scales the artwork up slightly from its center — use this to crop out any thin white sliver at the edge of the circle instead of nudging position.

Position tweak — top & bottom text

Radius now only changes the curve of the arc (300–480 px, 0.5 px steps); it no longer shifts the text up or down. Position moves the lettering up/down independently of the curve.

Name color

Starts as a golden maple-syrup amber. Pick a different base hue with the color swatch, then use the slider to lighten or darken it without changing the hue.

Print calibration — Avery 94510

Sheet shift moves the entire label grid together; use it to center the circles on your physical Avery sheet. Per Avery's official spec (0.5" left/right margin, 0.625" top/bottom margin), nominal row pitch is 2.5" and column gap is 0.375". Print at Actual Size / 100%.
