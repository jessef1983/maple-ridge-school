  • Measure from the siding (board face, not batten), and stretch string lines off the walls rather than trusting one corner: the building was measured with magicplan plus field corrections, so check it's square first (outside diagonals should both be 33'-0 1/2").
  • East awning line: string a line 6'-0" off the east face, parallel to it. A1-A4 sit on it at 7'-3 5/8" centers. A1 lines up with the existing post row at the south edge of the center bay. A4's outside face is flush with the north wall line.
  • Porch front line: 8'-0" off the north face. Check: NW corner to N4 and NE corner to N1 should both read 20'-10 5/8".
  • N6 / N7 sit tight to the north siding, centered 1 1/2" outside each wall line (under the purlins). Freestanding on their own footing, lagged to the corner frame for lateral tie only.
  • Tubes: hole about 3' deep, tube hung a few inches off the bottom so the concrete flows out into a 20-24" base; brace the tube at grade (engineer, 2026-11-04). Piers: pier footings are still pending the engineer - stake the centers now, size later.
  • The trench drain (12" x 43", hydrant to arch) and the French drain along the east drip line are dug with the Phase 1 footings; keep the drain trench clear of the tube holes.
