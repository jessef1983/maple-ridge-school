Cut notes:
- **P1:** notch the top outside face 2" × 9¼" for the eave beam. Bottom sits on a 1" standoff base. Rot-resistant species or PT.
- **P2/P3:** cut 3" long; trim to final length from the beam line after the bluestone caps are set. The listed nominal (64 7/16" / 71⅜") assumes the cap top at exactly 30" plus a 1" standoff base.
- **P4:** freestanding on its own footing; set tight to the siding and lag through to the existing corner frame for lateral tie only.
- **R1:** birdsmouth at the existing beam (7½" seat, 1⅞" heel) and at the eave beam (3" seat, ¾" heel); 2' tail.
- **R2:** one piece from the ridge to the tail at 3:12: plumb cut at the ridge, birdsmouth on the purlin and on the eave beam, 2' tail. Each one sits on the awning's 24" grid, plus a pair tight to the north wall and a fly pair at the front edge.
- **B1/K1:** splices land over posts 2 and 3, staggered between plies.
- **D1:** end joints over rafters; best face down (it's the ceiling).
